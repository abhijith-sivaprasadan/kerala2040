## Critical literature decision after this initial scan

The **IEEE 2026 Indian-states daily weather-demand paper** is the most important newly identified potential prior-art overlap. A journal introduction claiming novelty *because we combine Indian state electricity demand and weather* is currently **unjustified**. Before locking the hypothesis, obtain its full text lawfully and tabulate:

| Detail | IEEE 2026 abstract currently supports | Kerala2040 proposed work | Required validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Indian **states**, not necessarily Kerala-only | Kerala statewide operating boundary | Verify if their results include Kerala and if state-level weather covariate is local |
| Electricity source | Grid-India PSP daily published values | Qualified original Kerala SLDC operating consumption | Prove these reporting scopes actually differ in relevant way |
| Weather | Open-Meteo archive | ERA5 original-source at five points | Need a new methodological insight; merely using ERA5 instead of Open-Meteo is weak novelty |
| Study window | April 2023–October 2025 | SLDC original FY2020–25+ registered chronology | Verify all multi-year original ERA5 before claiming extra coverage |
| Evaluation | **Unknown from abstract** | Declared expanding-origin per-FY RMSE/MAE and exposure ledger | Compare exact models/partitions/availability of unseen test data from full paper |
| Scientific contribution | Regional weather dependency discussed | Provenance-driven test of weather-added incremental retrospective skill | Show quantitatively why improved source/validation changes an interpretable conclusion, not only code detail |
