## How to use this queue

1. Open the article DOI and obtain the version to which you have lawful access; record publisher metadata/version and downloaded PDF SHA256.
2. Match the paper's DOI and title to the **screening matrix**; flag wrong article or publication version rather than silently substitute.
3. Keep third-party original PDFs outside public GitHub, CI and release archives absent express redistribution rights.
4. Complete Round 2/3 analyses before executing Round 4 full-PDF appraisal; current Round 4 records are *screened*, not critically reviewed.
5. After reading, add methods/figures/data sample references, event vs simulated/observed distinction, transferability and independent reproducibility outcome.

For DOI-only tools, use [DOIS_FOR_UNPAYWALL.txt](DOIS_FOR_UNPAYWALL.txt); source classifications and individual questions are in [SCREENING_MATRIX.csv](SCREENING_MATRIX.csv).
