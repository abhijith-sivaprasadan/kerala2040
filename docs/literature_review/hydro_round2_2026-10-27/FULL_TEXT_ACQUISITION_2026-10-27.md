## Priority scientific comparison

Read **Ryan (Kakki/Pamba)** alongside **Sudheer (Periyar)**, not as a direct contradiction without controls. Their basins, reservoir boundaries, rainfall inputs, counterfactual rule curves, and modelled time/flow endpoints differ. Sudheer et al. (2019) and others have published disputed interpretations/author responses: [author response](https://sandrp.in/2020/09/19/response-of-sudheer-et-al-to-the-comments-by-mr-j-harsha-on-the-article-role-of-dams-on-the-floods-of-aug-2018-in-periyar-river-basin-kerala/). Keep hydrological flood attenuation distinct from MW/MWh electricity generation.

**Audit sequence:** Ryan 11 pages → Sudheer 15 pages (after source acquisition) → Suresh 56-page accepted manuscript → Singh → Das. For each: retain official DOI and version, source hash, every page inspection including figures/appendices, check model assumptions and rainfall bias correction, quantify flow units and temporal resolution, evaluate uncertainty and code/data release, and record the inability to reproduce without original inputs. Add comparison matrix only after audits.
