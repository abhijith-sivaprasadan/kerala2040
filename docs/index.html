FROM ONE SITE TO A SYSTEM

2030 model sensitivity

What changes when imports cost more?
What happens when transfer is constrained?

108 full-year PyPSA cases now test demand, renewable limits, battery costs, transfer limits and import-price assumptions. Explore selected published results below.

These are bounded research experiments, not Kerala’s recommended capacity plan. Existing generation is replayed; reservoir dispatch, outages, landed hourly prices and legal siting are not yet fully represented.

How to read the model and cost assumptions

The model first minimises unserved energy, then candidate investment plus import-energy cost. Costs share a real FY2021–22 price basis. The delivered-bulk price is a stress case, not a Kerala-border import tariff. Transfer sensitivities are 100%, 80% and 60% of the dated 4,455 MW ATC; future import requirements are not future transfer capacity.

The 2040 project horizon contains a nearer-term 2030 screening step. Numerical agreement between solvers verifies the formulation; it does not validate the underlying scenario assumptions.
