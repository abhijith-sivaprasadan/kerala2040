## Executive scientific assessment

The paper provides a useful **conceptual framework** for cross-sector resilience of water and electricity systems: absorptive, restorative and adaptive capacities; multi-timescale disruptions; diagrams of mutual dependencies; drought/heatwave resilience options; and an influence diagram linking joint strategies, impacts, costs and recovery. Yet **it does not implement or solve a water–power resilience optimization problem**, publish calibrated probabilities, quantify avoided service loss, or investigate Kerala electricity/hydrological records. The abstract's reference to jointly optimizing a strategy portfolio must not be read as a documented numerical demonstration.

### What its five figures show

1. **PDF p4, Figure 1:** institutional distribution along energy-vs-water and quantitative-vs-qualitative axes, with bubble size for work-product count. No numerical location scores. Text calls balanced studies *away from the centre*, in tension with the central balance interpretation.
2. **PDF p5, Figure 2:** a binary institution–research-topic matrix; does not measure rigor, publications by topic or impact. Underlying Table S1 is outside the provided PDF.
3. **PDF p9, Figure 3:** blue water and red electricity dependency links, **not** a physical topological or calibrated flow model.
4. **PDF p10, Figure 4:** illustrative drought/heatwave links and candidate capacity/nontraditional-water/microgrid responses, no optimized benefits.
5. **PDF p12, Figure 5:** influence graph with decisions, uncertainties and evaluation. No objective or hydraulic/electrical constraints, scenario probabilities or optimized solutions.

**Search evidence distinction:** Approximately 100 DOE technical reports and 30 external institutions were explored; these are distinct units of analysis, **not 130 fully reviewed peer-reviewed papers**. Search language and selection protocol are insufficient for a reproducible systematic review. The numbered bibliography has **75 references**, whose underlying documents have not been individually audited in this review.
