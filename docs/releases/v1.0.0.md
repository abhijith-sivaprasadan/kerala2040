# Kerala 2040 v1.0.0 — Data Platform / Historical Foundation

First reproducible public release of the Kerala 2040 **data platform and historical research foundation**. This is **not** a validated 2040 electricity expansion study or a policy recommendation.

## Included

- Kerala SLDC FY2024–25 observed-day system balance and reservoir chronology, hydro/storage/import diagnostics, and an observed-day PyPSA energy replay.
- Explicit reconciliation of SLDC, CEA and KSEBL/Economic Review energy-accounting boundaries; official totals with different definitions are not silently equated.
- Source-labelled KSEB project inventory, NASA POWER weather/resource modelling code, ERA5 acquisition status and GIS acquisition register.
- CSTEP 2024 demand, supply and resource estimates as **external published benchmarks**, not this project's 2040 results.
- Public dashboard with provenance-labelled charts, dated evidence, downloadable derived tables and synthetic-data publication safeguards.

## Scientific limitations

- The SLDC baseline currently covers **354 of 365 days** in FY2024–25; the **11 missing days are not measured observations**. Daily energy-accounting validation passes, but **hourly/15-minute historical calibration does not**.
- The separate 8,760-hour demand reconstruction is **SYNTHETIC / NOT MEASURED**, including interpolated missing daily totals. It is excluded from the public Pages release. The observed-day PyPSA replay is **not** an hourly dispatch or capacity-expansion solution.
- Solar/wind availability is **weather-derived, modelled resource availability**, not measured plant generation. NASA POWER is representative-point weather; ERA5 coverage is partial.
- Reservoir correlations do not establish causality or a validated future hydro operating constraint. The current import-interface normalisation is **not a verified transfer limit**.
- Downloaded KSDMA flood inputs and GIS catalogues are **not processed ecological exclusions or capacity ceilings**. Plant-level generation, grid constraints, final technology costs, hydro inflow futures, finance, reliability and KMML mass/energy balances remain incomplete.
- There is **no validated 2040 optimal technology mix, storage requirement, import-reduction result or cost forecast** in this release.

The downloadable site archive includes `SOURCE_COMMIT` and `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` recording the immutable release commit, the source evidence snapshot timestamp/hash and the publication policy. See `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md` for the remaining research gates.
