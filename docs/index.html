01 THE IDEA / ആശയം

This is not a forecast.
It is a way to ask better questions.

Kerala2040 is an open, independent research project building a reproducible picture of the state’s energy system—and testing what could change by 2040. It connects electricity demand and imports with hydropower, renewable resources, land, ecological protection, industrial value and public finance.

Energy resilience is not zero imports. The goal is to understand what Kerala should be able to control, what it can sensibly trade, where infrastructure can actually go and how choices hold up in a difficult monsoon or dry year.

THE INTERPRETATION

Dependence is a system question.

Daily energy imports, transfer capacity and contracted power are not interchangeable. The project separates these quantities before examining storage, hydro flexibility, demand management or new generation.

One year of observations is not enough to establish an hourly dispatch model. Read the missing-date and interval-data limits before using these numbers for planning.

04 തൊഴിൽ / CIRCULAR INDUSTRY

Keep more value

Kerala’s energy future also concerns process heat, water, chemicals, waste, products and work—not only megawatts.

A resource is only recovered when the balance closes.

Starting with KMML and other documented industrial cases, the project separates operational evidence from proposed recovery routes. No recovery tonnes, economic returns or avoided emissions are invented for facilities lacking measured flows.
