# Primary circular-industry case: KMML, Chavara

User-confirmed on 18 September 2026. TTPL and FACT remain comparison references.
This facility case sits inside the whole-Kerala study; it does not narrow the
electricity model to Chavara or Kochi.

## First deliverable

A sourced material/energy/water flow diagram and baseline residual-stream ledger.
Then shortlist a recovery or reuse route with a mass balance and cost range.

## Evidence needed for a consistent reporting period

- Process boundaries, throughput, feedstocks and products.
- Residual/by-product quantities, chemistry, moisture and regulatory classification.
- Existing treatment, disposal, transport and recovery routes.
- Electricity, fuels, process heat and water use aligned with production periods.
- Disposal costs, recovery yields, energy/reagent needs, investment/operating costs
  and credible product offtake specifications.
- Permission to publish raw or aggregated data and derived findings.

Use official annual reports, environmental/consent records and facility-confirmed
information. Public evidence is partial. Do not transfer TTPL sulphate-route
recovery figures to KMML or assume interchangeable process chemistry.

Compare the existing route with prevention/reuse and a justified recovery option.
Report tonnes in/out, contaminants, energy/water balance, residual disposal,
avoided costs, offtake uncertainty and who pays/benefits. Separate planned projects
from commissioned operations. Select a technology only after characterisation.
