## Download workflow

1. Open a round above, then click the **publication title** for its DOI landing page (or title search for R02).
2. Where present, click the **PDF candidate** to try the recorded PDF URL; otherwise the full-text lead/DOI page may offer open access, a university subscription login, or a request link.
3. Before using an article in Kerala2040 research, verify its exact DOI, title, year, publication type, version and numerical/figure evidence. The previously supplied original PDFs are separately preserved in private source archives; this public bibliographic index does not redistribute those PDFs.

**Caution:** User-provided PDFs for R02–R05 do not make the 808-paper DOI discovery catalogue a full-text-reviewed corpus. R02's 40 title-only scoping rows have no DOI in this atlas; the link is therefore an explicit title search rather than a guessed publisher identifier.
