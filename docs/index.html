TRACEABILITY

What this audit does — and does not — establish

The checks inspect the research repository’s committed evidence. They cannot establish what is held in private utility records, local unpublished archives, or external workflow artifacts. Missing here means not validated within this published research snapshot, not that the information does not exist.
