Subject: Research data request — Kerala state demand and actual interchange, FY2024–25

Dear Chief Engineer,

I am [name; affiliation, if applicable], developing Kerala 2040, an independent
statewide energy-systems research project for a CET 2026 presentation. The study
covers the entire Kerala electricity system: demand, generation, interstate
dependence, hydro flexibility, storage and ecological constraints. It does not
claim KSEBL or Government endorsement.

We have assembled public daily SLDC statistics and would like to calibrate a
historical chronological model against authenticated state-level observations.
Could your office provide, or direct us to the custodian of, the following
existing electronic records for 1 April 2024–31 March 2025?

1. Kerala state demand/load in MW, preferably 15-minute interval averages; hourly
   averages are also useful. Please identify demand met versus unrestricted demand,
   and how losses and embedded generation are treated.
2. Actual aggregate interstate imports and exports/drawal in MW, separately if
   available, or net interchange with its sign convention and measurement boundary.
3. Timestamp/block number, interval length, timezone, interval-start/end labelling,
   missing/revised-data flags and a data dictionary. Please distinguish interval
   average MW, instantaneous MW and interval energy in MWh.

If readily available, we also welcome scheduled drawal (separate from actual),
aggregate in-state generation by source, curtailment/load shedding, and the
identities of interfaces included in the aggregate. Detailed topology, protection
settings and operational security information are not requested.

CSV or Excel is preferred, in your existing export format. A complete FY2024–25
series contains 8,760 hourly or 35,040 fifteen-minute intervals. A short sample
and data dictionary first would help confirm interpretation. If that year cannot
be supplied, please indicate the nearest complete financial year available.

State aggregates are sufficient initially. Please specify attribution, research-use
conditions and whether raw observations or only derived figures may be published.
We will respect these conditions and can share the reconciliation method and
derived findings for technical feedback.

Please advise whether a formal application, institutional letter, fee or data-use
agreement is required, and which officer should coordinate the request.
