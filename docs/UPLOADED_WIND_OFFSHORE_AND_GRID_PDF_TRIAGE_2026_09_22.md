## Corrected source relationships

- The previous [Anna University Tirunelveli grid evacuation review](UPLOADED_ANNA_UNIVERSITY_WIND_EVACUATION_TRIAGE_2026_09_22.md) should **not** group power-quality Table 5.1 / E2 feeder tables with Anna University. They are now **source-attributed to the separately uploaded March 2014 power-quality report**, subject to each row/page quality check. The documented Anna University **2.25 vs extracted 92.25 MW** substation error remains valid.
- The previous [loose wind CSV triage](UPLOADED_WIND_CSV_TRIAGE_2026_09_22.md) correctly rejected Gujarat LiDAR as Kerala evidence; the full **2018 NIWE report** now independently confirms Gujarat provenance. The 2016 NIWE hybrid report is not a Kerala station dataset.
- All offshore documents apart from the dated 2026 SRPC agenda address **Gujarat, Tamil Nadu or non-Kerala sites**. Offshore wind for Kerala stays a **missing-resource and legal/marine-grid assessment**, not an automatically admitted onshore-to-offshore multiplier.
- Rights are source-specific: avoid public mirroring of NIWE/C-WET PDFs, tables, scanned pages, original maritime EIA figures or NIWE-derived maps until their terms are resolved; attribution of FOWPI documents does not automatically grant NIWE source-data rights.
