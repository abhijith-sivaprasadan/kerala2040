# Kerala 2040 v1.0.1 — FY2024–25 expanded SLDC daily evidence

This post-v1.0.0 data update publishes **user-acquired official daily SLDC public reports**, first-pass normalised CSV tables, quality checks and a new dashboard evidence explorer. The original 16.7 MB raw HTML archive was supplied for this integration and retained by the researcher; the public download is the compact processed evidence ZIP, whose QA report identifies the SHA-256 of the original archive and each report's SHA-256.

## Added

- 354 observed dates in the 365-day FY2024–25 chronology across five public SLDC sections: Generation, Imports, Storage, Availability / Merit Order, and Others / demand extrema.
- Source-labelled CSVs for 5,779 daily hydro-station rows, 2,832 import-interface rows, 5,664 reservoir rows, 3,540 selected intraday extrema and a 365-row daily balance with 11 explicit missing dates.
- Station and interface energy summaries, observed monthly hydro/import totals and reservoir storage ranges available as downloadable JSON and illustrated on the Electricity dashboard.
- Raw archive digest and per-observation source-response hashes, and consistency checks against the official observed-day aggregate balance.

## Do not infer

Daily station outputs are **not hourly dispatch or commissioned generator MW**; reported interface energies are **not MW transfer capability**. Blank station cells are unknown, not zero. The missing 11 days are not interpolated. The reservoir curves do not identify verified hydraulic cascades or permit independent summation of gross hydro-generation capability. The intraday extrema are isolated observations and do not supply a 15-minute or 8,760-hour measured load chronology. The project's 2040 expansion model remains uncalibrated and unsolved.

The updated website build retains the immutable source commit in `SOURCE_COMMIT` and `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`; the v1.0.0 tag is not moved.
