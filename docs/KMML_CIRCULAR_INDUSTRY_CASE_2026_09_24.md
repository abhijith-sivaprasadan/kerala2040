## 1. The research question and what we established

**Question:** At a fully integrated Kerala chloride-route titanium pigment facility, which material, heat and water loops already exist, which residual streams have independently distinguishable recovery hypotheses, and what must be measured to establish *net* resource, energy and economic benefit?

**Answer supported by the evidence reviewed here:** KMML publicly describes physical ilmenite beneficiation, acid regeneration, TiCl4 chlorination, TiCl4 oxidation and pigment finishing, with a separate titanium-sponge product branch. A recovered-HCl loop and an iron-oxide by-product route form a real **company-described existing circular-process topology**. The company’s **FY2022–23 annual report** separately records iron-oxide-to-sponge-iron *trials*, pigment-fines settling *trials* and filter-backwash reuse *trials*, while describing permanent installations as proposed/in progress. Thus the opportunity is not a hypothetical new industry: it is to **measure and improve existing loops and establish which trial recovery routes were actually deployed**. None of those statements establishes annual project returns, current pollution compliance or a causal emissions reduction.

## 5. Residual-stream ledger — ten distinct entries

The machine-readable [case registry](../data/evidence/industry/kmml_source_bounded_case_2026_09_24.json) contains **ten** labelled material/energy/water streams: iron oxide, recovered acid, spent liquor, U200 impurity/purge material, chlorine loop, pigment fines, backwash water, ETP sludge, heat opportunity and TSP MgCl2. Every stream has a *source*, a *physical type*, a documented status, minimum new measurements and explicit `null` values for annual tonnes, annual MWh and avoided CO2. A `null` is **not zero**.

| Distinction that changes the answer | Never substitute |
|---|---|

| Raw TiO2 slurry vs dry finished grade mix | Nominal TiO2 capacity or paper pigment quality |

## 6. Reproducible engineering calculation framework (no hypothetical answer passed off as fact)

Define a *common reporting period* for all flow meters, assays, energy invoices and product sales; measure inventory change across its endpoints.

**6A / Chloride–iron accounting.** For each measured material boundary, calculate elemental Fe and Cl inventories, including Fe(II)/Fe(III), acid product and scrubbing/purge. Schematic, chemically balanced pure-component reaction templates (not yields):

```text
4 FeCl2 + 4 H2O + O2 -> 2 Fe2O3 + 8 HCl
2 FeCl3 + 3 H2O      ->   Fe2O3 + 6 HCl
```

Actual recovered HCl mass = mass of recovered acid solution × **measured** HCl mass fraction. Source text’s reported total chloride % is **not** acid-product wt% and must not be substituted. Actual acid avoidance must be shown against fresh-acid purchases, inventories and leak/purge streams while subtracting additional fuel and electricity for recovery.

**6B / Pigment fines.** Annual (or other period) input to *a precisely metered U400 overflow stream*:

```text
TiO2 in overflow (kg) = sum over periods [
  U400 overflow volume (m³) × measured representative solids (g/L)
]
Saleable recovered TiO2 = dry solids captured × analytically accepted grade fraction
```

Unit identity: **1 g/L = 1 kg/m³**. The **FY2022–23 self-reported 1–2 g/L** alone gives **1–2 kg per measured m³**, before capturing/grade/reject losses; it does *not* yield any t/year without U400-specific Q, uptime and time-matched C. PAC additions can create separate chemical sludge; that is not pigment recovery. The other FY2022–23 figure, 300 m³/day, is **filter backwash**, not that runoff; **do not multiply the two**.

**6C / Water reuse.** Freshwater saved = net accepted backwash reused as cooling make-up − any additional fresh water used in treatment. Prove cooling-water salinity/conductivity, fouling/scale, microbial controls if applicable, PAC residual, discharge and measured substitution. Report backwash generated, backwash **treated**, reuse accepted and freshwater actually displaced **separately**.

**6D / Heat.** Match time-series source temperature/flow and sink temperature/flow; account for minimum temperature approach, fouling, corrosive composition, pressure-drop pumping, equipment availability and marginal fuel displaced. Site steam claims are **nameplate descriptions**; they do not measure recoverable MWh.

**6E / Economics and emissions.** Net incremental benefit = verified avoided purchased acid/water/fuel + accepted product revenue + avoided compliant disposal − *incremental* energy/reagent/transport/quality-control/residual disposal/O&M − annualised capital. Exclude cash transfers within the same owned boundary and double-counted credits. Emissions require source-specific, marginal **displaced** supply and added emissions; neither “every recovered tonne avoids a tonne of CO2” nor a renewable-energy claim follows from topology.

## 7. Constrained experiment design: measure before proposing technology

| Question / test | Minimum measurement and QA | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| **ARP:** fresh-acid displacement? | Same-month spent liquor species/tonnes, recovered HCl wt%, purchased HCl, rejects and roaster oil/electricity | Existing-loop efficiency, not an unverified new ARP |
| **Oxide:** saleable brick/iron? | Representative *fresh and stored* oxide fractions, chloride/acidity/moisture/metals, process energy, buyer melt/brick tests, residue/transport | Whether oxide is a verified co-product rather than merely relocated waste |
| **U400 fines:** useful capture? | U400-specific composite C and flow over the same period, PAC dose, dry recoverable solids and saleable grade | Net pigment recovery vs additional sludge |
| **Filter backwash:** accepted reuse? | Date-matched backwash generation, water-quality assay, cooling make-up acceptance, purge, chemical use, metered return | True freshwater displacement |
| **Heat:** displaced utility fuel? | Concurrent source/sink temperature and flow, actual steam/fuel meters and uptime | Net useful heat and feasibility |
| **ETP:** compliant outlet and sludge? | As-issued KSPCB/CPCB conditions, laboratory effluent/stack results, actual solid movement, disposal certificates | Independent compliance and disposal, not a website statement |

**Publication and confidentiality:** Ask KMML to approve at least anonymised **monthly aggregated** chemistry, energy, water and output for the same 12 months, with meter definitions and rights to publish derived quantities; retain raw sensitive plant rows privately. Do not publish unreviewed P&IDs, source scans, detailed safety-critical process settings or trade-secret operating data.
